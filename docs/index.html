Donna

A local-first personal assistant for macOS. Tasks, goals, habits, notes and a chat that actually knows your world — powered by your own AI key.

Private by default

Your data never leaves your Mac

No account, no cloud sync, no telemetry. Your tasks, notes, goals and tracked time live in ~/Library/Application Support/Donna. AI requests go straight from your Mac to the provider you choose — nothing in between.

  • Bring your own key (Anthropic · OpenAI · MiniMax)
  • Or use the local claude CLI
  • Works offline for everything except Ask
  • Export a full backup any time
